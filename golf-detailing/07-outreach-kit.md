# 07 — Club Outreach Kit

Everything you need to go from "cold" to a signed pilot club. Templates are written for
you (Luke) to send/say. Swap the `[brackets]`. Pair every outreach with the one-pager in
`04-club-partnership-proposal.md`.

---

## Who to contact (in priority order)

1. **General Manager / COO** — owns the member experience and can say yes to an amenity.
   Primary target.
2. **Membership Director** — motivated by member perks and retention; great champion.
3. **Head Golf Professional** — controls the tee sheet and bag-drop area; strong ally,
   sometimes the fastest path in at a golf-first club.

**Avoid leading with:** the pro-shop desk or F&B — they'll punt you.

## How to find the right name

- Club website → "About/Staff/Contact" page usually lists the GM and directors.
- LinkedIn: search `[Club Name] General Manager` / `Membership Director`.
- Call the main line: *"Hi, I'm putting together a member-amenity proposal — who's the
  best person to send that to, the GM or membership director?"* Get name + email.

## Touch 1 — Intro email (short, curiosity + amenity framing)

**Subject:** A new member perk for [Club Name] — zero cost to the club

> Hi [Name],
>
> I run Fairway Detail Co. — we detail members' cars **on-site while they play their
> round**. They drop their keys at the bag drop, tee off, and drive home to a car that
> looks new. No waiting, no errand, no leaving the club.
>
> We set it up as a **free amenity for your members at no cost or staff burden to the
> club** — fully insured, uniformed technicians, and a turf-safe setup that captures all
> water runoff so there's zero impact on your grounds.
>
> I'd love to offer [Club Name] a **free 30-day pilot** so your members can try it. Could
> I grab 20 minutes to show you how it works?
>
> Best,
> Luke [Last] — Fairway Detail Co.
> [phone] • [email] • [website]

## Touch 2 — Follow-up (2–3 business days later, attach the proposal)

**Subject:** Re: A new member perk for [Club Name]

> Hi [Name], following up with the one-page overview attached.
>
> Quick version: premium detailing done in your lot while members golf, positioned as
> *your* amenity, fully insured and turf-safe, at **no cost to the club** — with an
> optional revenue share if you'd like it as a small income line.
>
> Happy to run a no-obligation 30-day pilot. Would [day] or [day] work for a quick call
> or a walk of the bag-drop area?
>
> Luke • [phone]

## Touch 3 — Phone / voicemail script

> "Hi [Name], this is Luke with Fairway Detail Co. I sent a note about detailing members'
> cars on-site while they golf — a free amenity for your members, no cost to the club,
> fully insured and turf-safe. I'd love 20 minutes to show you how it works. I'm at
> [phone] — thanks!"

## Touch 4 — On-site 20-minute walkthrough (the close)

Bring: the one-pager, insurance summary, a photo/short video of the rig + water-reclaim
setup, and sample before/after photos.
Walk the bag-drop and lot with them. Talking points:
- Where we'd stage (out of the way, clean, quiet).
- The member flow on a golf day (keys at bag drop → detail → keys back).
- How runoff is captured (show the reclamation mat) — reassure grounds crew.
- The pilot: free for 30 days, we comp the GM/head pro's cars and hand you a stack of
  member gift certificates to give out.
- **Ask for the pilot start date.** ("Could we run the pilot the first two Saturdays next
  month?")

## Objection handling

| They say… | You say… |
|-----------|----------|
| "Members might think it's some random guy in the lot." | "That's exactly why we present it as *your* amenity — uniformed, background-checked, insured techs, co-branded however you want. It reflects the club, not a vendor." |
| "What about the turf / runoff / environmental rules?" | "We use a water-reclamation mat that captures 100% of runoff and pH-neutral, turf-safe products. Nothing hits your grounds or storm drains. Happy to show your superintendent." |
| "Liability if a car gets damaged?" | "We carry garagekeepers (care, custody & control) plus general liability, and we name the club as additional insured. You'll have our certificate on file before we ever touch a car." |
| "We don't want to manage another vendor." | "You don't manage anything. Members book with us directly or sign up at the bag drop; we handle scheduling, service, and payment. You just get the credit for a great perk." |
| "What does it cost us?" | "Nothing. It's free to the club. If you'd like, we can add a 10–15% revenue share so it's actually a small income line for the club." |
| "How do I know members want it?" | "That's what the free 30-day pilot answers — no commitment. If members don't love it, you've lost nothing." |
