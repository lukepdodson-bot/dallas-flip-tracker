# Fairway Detail Co. — Business Setup Workspace

**Concept:** On-site mobile car detailing for golf & country club members. We detail
the member's car in the club lot while they play their round. They drop keys at the
bag drop, tee off, and drive home in a car that looks new. Booking is direct with the
member or arranged through the club as a member amenity.

**Status:** Pre-launch setup. Market validated (see `01-market-validation.md`).
Legal entity, books, and club-ownership conversations are owned by Luke. Everything
else (brand, model, pricing, partnership pitch, go-to-market, ops, launch) is built out
here.

> "Fairway Detail Co." is the working name — see `02-brand-and-positioning.md` for the
> shortlist. Swap it repo-wide once you pick.
