How It Works

Detailed while you golf. That's it.

No scheduling drop-off windows, no second trip to a car wash. Four simple steps built around your tee time.

  1. 01

    Book to match your tee time

    Reserve your detail online or through your club — we schedule around when you're actually playing.

  2. 02

    Drop keys at the bag drop

    Hand off your keys the way you always do. Our technician checks in and gets to work immediately.

  3. 03

    We detail in the lot while you play

    Your car is hand-detailed on-site in the member lot — turf-safe products, full water reclamation.

  4. 04

    Drive home brand new

    Finish your round, grab your keys at the bag drop, and drive home in a freshly detailed car.
