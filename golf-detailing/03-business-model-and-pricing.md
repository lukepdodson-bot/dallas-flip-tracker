# 03 — Business Model, Service Menu & Pricing

The model is built around one insight: **the length of the round is the length of the
service window.** We tier the menu to how long the member will be away.

## Service menu

| Tier | What's included | Fits a… | Target price |
|------|-----------------|---------|--------------|
| **The Turn** (express) | Hand wash, wheels/tires, quick interior vacuum + wipe-down, windows | 9 holes / quick round (~2 hrs) | **$75–95** |
| **The Full Round** ⭐ | Everything in The Turn + full interior detail (seats, carpets, console, leather condition), exterior wash + spray wax, tire dressing | 18 holes (~4–4.5 hrs) | **$185–245** |
| **The Championship** | Full Round + paint enhancement/one-step polish, clay bar, premium sealant | 18 holes + lunch, or scheduled premium slot | **$350–450** |
| **Add-ons** | Ceramic coating, headlight restoration, pet-hair, engine bay, odor treatment | Booked ahead (may need a return visit) | à la carte |

⭐ **The Full Round** is the hero product — priced and timed to the 18-hole member, the
core customer.

## The recurring-revenue engine: membership plans

This is what turns a one-off detail into a business. It maps perfectly onto club
psychology (members already pay monthly dues).

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Clubhouse** | **$99/mo** | 1 Turn express per month + priority booking |
| **Members'** ⭐ | **$159/mo** | 1 Full Round per month + priority booking + 10% off add-ons |
| **Championship** | **$279/mo** | 1 Full Round + 1 Turn per month + first-in-line + 15% off add-ons |

Goal: convert a meaningful share of active members onto a monthly plan. Predictable
revenue lets us schedule techs efficiently against the tee sheet.

## Unit economics (single tech, conservative)

**Per Full Round detail (~$210 avg):**
- Supplies/consumables: ~$12
- Labor (tech at ~$22/hr × ~2.5 hrs, or per-car commission ~$45–55): ~$50
- Water/power (own tank + generator): ~$3
- **Contribution per car ≈ $145 (~65% gross margin)**

**Per tech, per golf day** (prime tee times ~7 a.m.–1 p.m.):
- Realistic mix: 2 Full Rounds + 2 Turns ≈ **$520–680 revenue/day**
- Contribution ≈ **$330–430/day** before fixed costs
- One tech, ~4 club-days/week ≈ **$2,100–2,700 revenue/week**

**Fixed monthly (lean):** insurance, software, phone, fuel, misc ≈ **$400–700/mo.**

**Takeaway:** A single well-utilized tech at one active club clears fixed costs quickly;
profit is a function of *booked utilization*, which the membership plans and the club's
tee sheet make predictable. Scale = more techs and more clubs, not more marketing spend.

## How the club gets paid (options to negotiate — Luke owns this convo)

Structured so the club says yes easily. Pick per club:
1. **Free member amenity + revenue share** — club takes 10–15% of revenue booked through
   them. Zero cost to club, new perk, small income stream. *(Usually the easiest yes.)*
2. **Flat monthly amenity fee** to the club for exclusive on-property rights.
3. **Comped details** for the GM, head pro, and key staff + a set of member gift
   certificates the club can use as perks. *(Great for the pilot / goodwill.)*

Default opening offer for a pilot: **option 3 during a free 30-day pilot**, then move to
**option 1** once demand is proven.

## Booking flow

- **Direct:** member books via our online scheduler (pick club + date, we match it to
  their tee time). Text confirmations.
- **Through the club:** sign-up at the bag drop / pro shop, or via the club's app/
  concierge; club passes us the list.
- **On-site capture:** a clean sign-up card at bag drop for walk-up demand while we're
  on property — this is where a lot of first jobs come from.
