# Caddy 19
### A Member Amenity Proposal for [Club Name]

**You play. We detail. Drive home brand new.**

---

Caddy 19 brings premium, on-site car detailing to your members while they play — dropping keys at the bag drop before their round and picking up a freshly detailed car after it. There is no cost to the club, no staff burden, and no change to your operations. It's simply a five-star amenity that makes membership feel a little more like membership.

---

## Why Members Love It

- **Time they don't have to spend.** A detail that would eat a Saturday afternoon happens automatically during a round they were already playing.
- **Zero disruption to their day.** No drop-off trip, no waiting, no second stop — the car is simply better than they left it.
- **Club-caliber quality.** Hand detailing, not a drive-through wash — the same standard of care members expect from everything else at the club.
- **Effortless to book.** A quick sign-up before tee time, and it's handled.
- **A genuine "wow" moment.** Walking to a spotless car after 18 holes is the kind of small luxury members mention to their guests.

## Why It's Good for the Club

- **A differentiated amenity** few clubs in DFW currently offer — a tangible point of difference in member recruiting and retention conversations.
- **Zero cost, zero staffing.** We bring our own team, equipment, and insurance. Nothing is asked of your pro shop or grounds crew beyond a small footprint in the lot.
- **Optional revenue share.** If you'd like the amenity to generate income rather than simply be a perk, a revenue-share structure is available (details below).
- **Exclusivity available.** The partnership can be structured so Caddy 19 is the club's exclusive detailing provider — no competing vans soliciting members in your lot.
- **Low-risk to try.** The pilot below is completely free, so there's no commitment required to see how members respond.

## How It Works

1. **Bag drop.** The member hands off their keys at the bag drop, same as valet.
2. **Tee off.** Our uniformed technician moves the vehicle to a designated staging area and begins work immediately.
3. **On the course.** Over the guaranteed 4–4.5 hour window of a round, the full service is completed — inside and out, no rush, no shortcuts.
4. **19th hole.** Keys are returned to the bag drop (or pro shop, per club preference) before the member finishes up. The car is ready when they are.

**Booking options** — the club can offer whichever fits its style, or a combination:
- **Direct online scheduler** — members book a tier and time slot themselves, no club involvement required.
- **Pro shop sign-up** — a simple sheet or tablet at the counter when members check in for their round.
- **Club app / concierge integration** — booked alongside tee times for clubs with a member app or concierge desk.
- **Bag-drop card** — a physical card members simply hand to the attendant to opt in on the spot.

## How We Protect Your Club & Turf

We understand our work happens on your property, in front of your members, and we treat that as a privilege.

- **Water-reclamation mats** are placed under every vehicle, capturing 100% of runoff — nothing reaches your turf, drains, or landscaping.
- **pH-neutral, turf-safe products** exclusively — no harsh chemicals, no risk to grass, mulch, or nearby plantings.
- **Fully insured**, with a certificate of insurance naming the club as additional insured, provided before we ever set foot on property.
- **Uniformed, background-checked technicians** who represent the professionalism your members expect.
- **A clean, self-contained rig** — no sprawling equipment, no mess left behind, no trace we were there except a spotless car.
- **Presented as the club's amenity**, co-branded with your name and colors where desired — this is your program, we're simply the team behind it.

## The Partnership

We keep this simple. Three ways to structure the relationship — pick what fits, change later if you'd like:

1. **Free member amenity + revenue share (10–15% to the club).** The easiest yes — zero cost, and the club earns a share of every booking. *(Ongoing default after the pilot.)*
2. **Flat monthly amenity fee.** For clubs that want Caddy 19 as their exclusive, dedicated provider.
3. **Comped details for staff + member gift certificates.** Complimentary details for your GM, head pro, and key staff, plus a set of gift certificates to gift or raffle to members. *(Recommended for the pilot below.)*

**Our recommendation:** we start with a **free 30-day pilot** under option 3 — no cost, no commitment, comped details for your leadership team so you can experience it firsthand, and gift certificates to introduce a handful of members. If it's a hit (it will be), we transition to option 1 as the ongoing arrangement.
